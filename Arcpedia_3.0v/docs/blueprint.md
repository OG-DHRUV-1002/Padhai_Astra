# **App Name**: DelphiAI

## Core Features:

- Personalized Dashboard: Provides a customizable overview of a student's academic progress, upcoming events, and important announcements.
- Smart Timetable: Automatically generates and optimizes a student's timetable based on course schedules, personal commitments, and study preferences.
- Attendance and Grade Tracking: Allows students to monitor their attendance records and grades for all courses, with visual representations of their academic performance.
- AI-Powered Resource Recommendation: Recommends relevant learning resources, study materials, and external websites based on a student's courses, academic performance, and learning style. The LLM acts as a tool that helps personalize the resources
- Interactive Quizzes: Provides a platform for students to take quizzes, review their answers, and receive immediate feedback on their understanding of the material.
- Community Forum: A community space for students to ask questions, share resources, and connect with peers in their courses.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Calm blue (#6699CC) to evoke a sense of trust and focus.
- Background color: Soft gray (#F0F0F0) for a clean and modern feel.
- Accent color: Muted orange (#D98880) to highlight key actions and information.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' sans-serif, for a computerized look. Body Font: 'Inter', sans-serif, to support longer passages of text.
- Use clear, minimalist icons to represent different academic areas and features.
- Employ a card-based layout for easy navigation and content organization.
- Use subtle transitions and animations to enhance the user experience and provide feedback.
